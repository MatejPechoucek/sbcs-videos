SBCS Previews

Offline renders of the mod's cutscenes, one per drop. They use the same drawing and sound code as the game, but over a stand-in sky instead of the real world, and some skins and prices are stand-ins. Tap a clip to play it with sound.

The Scatha: the cleaver

The original. The cleaver takes the worm apart one segment at a time, and only a pet drop reaches the head. Offline the worm wears the mod's stand-in skin; in game it is the real Scatha's.

The Thaw: Vanguard corpses

The pick chips the ice block by block. The last strike cycles blue, violet and gold before landing on the best drop's true colour, then every drop lands in the tray.

Diana: The Chopping Block

Every Minos kill drops its head on the block. Three chops, the cracks cycle colours, and only the split shows what dropped. Prices here are stand-ins.

Hellion: Inferno Demonlord

The blaze's shield cycles through its attunements and lands on the drop's. Grey is a bust. Prices here are stand-ins.

Eruption: lava fishing

The creature sinks, a vent rises, and its heat lands on the drop. Black is a bust. Prices here are stand-ins.

The Vault: Crystal Nucleus

Five crystals unlock the safe, the dial spins, and the door tells you how good the bundle was.

The Ossuary: dungeon chests

One big prize wheel for every floor's reward chest. Master Mode gets the red wheel with seals. Items are flat icons offline.

Fin: Fishing Festival

A quick pop-up, no cutscene: the fin rises, the cleaver comes down, and the drop pops out.

Wisp: Dwarven Mines ghosts

A charged ghost rises out of the mist and bursts into the drop.

Slayers: the Revenant (Exhumed)

Only good drops play, and each drop's ending is about its item.

Slayers: the Tarantula (The Brood)

Only good drops play, and each drop's ending is about its item.

Slayers: the Voidgloom Seraph

The Seraph throws its beacon and the beam's colour is the tell. A black beam is the bust.

Scatha pet tiers

Rare, Epic and Legendary each get their own reveal. Nothing before the landing gives the tier away. The worm wears the placeholder skin here.

Superpairs: The Hunt

The rare tile stays a black box in the menu. The cutscene shows it after the game. The heads are stand-in skins.

Lava fishing: Taurus and more

Taurus plays only for its own drops. Jawbus now has an entrance. The Helmet and Dye skins are stand-ins.

Titanoboa and Wiki Tiki

The Aquamarine Dye is the grand ending for both. Detection is built from wiki strings until records confirm it. The skins are stand-ins.

The Ledger

New chapters for the Revenant and the Tarantula, and the Seraph's chapter with its tape off. 12 stamps in each, some of them secret.
